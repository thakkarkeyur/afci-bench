## Objective

Order totals must be exact to the cent even for orders with many line items at
fractional unit prices. Ensure the computed totals are correct and consistent.

## User-visible behaviour

When an order is created, each line item's subtotal and the order's overall total
are rounded to two decimal places and are exact to the cent. Summing many
fractional-priced items does not introduce floating-point drift in the returned
totals.

## Inputs and outputs

- Input: the existing create-order request; no new request fields.
- Output: the existing create-order response, with cent-exact `subtotal` and
  `total` values.

## Constraints

- Two-decimal rounding is applied consistently across all subtotal and total
  calculations.
- For example, three items priced at 15.33 each yield a subtotal of 45.99, and an
  order combining many such items yields a total with no sub-cent drift.

## Functional completion criteria

- Orders with fractional unit prices return cent-exact subtotals and totals.
- Adding more line items does not accumulate rounding error in the total.

Run `npm run ci:agent`; it must pass.
